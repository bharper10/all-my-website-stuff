Tyler-Justin Anthony Sharpe, known professionally as Lil Tecca, is an American rapper, singer, and songwriter born on August 26, 2002, in Queens, New York. Lil Tecca began uploading his music to SoundCloud, which helped him gain initial traction. His career surged in 2019 with the release of his hit single "Ransom". The song, produced by Nick Mira and Taz Taylor, became a massive success, peaking at number four on the Billboard Hot 100 and accumulating hundreds of millions of streams. The music video, released on the Lyrical Lemonade YouTube channel, further amplified its reach. This single is widely considered his signature song.
